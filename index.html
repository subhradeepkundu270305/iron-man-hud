100% ON-DEVICE EDGE AI — NO VIDEO EVER LEAVES THIS BROWSER

One hand: point to rotate the target ring, pinch (thumb+index) to lock a system, hold your palm open to charge and fire a repulsor blast, make a fist to raise a shield, point with just your index finger to scan, and hold a peace sign to cycle the HUD view.
Two hands: pull apart or push together to zoom the HUD and charge the core.

What's actually happening: MediaPipe's HandLandmarker returns 21 3D points per hand, entirely in your browser (WASM + GPU delegate, no server round-trip). Pinch = distance between landmarks 4 and 8. Open-palm = average fingertip distance from the palm center. Fist / point / peace-sign = comparing how far each fingertip sits from the wrist versus its own knuckle — simple Math.hypot() math, no gesture model. Two-hand zoom = distance between the two palm centers, smoothed and mapped onto a CSS transform: scale() that scales the whole scene — including the suit hologram. The suit renders and the shield glyph are the images you supplied, compressed and embedded directly in this file as data URIs, so there's nothing to fetch and nothing external can go missing. All sound is synthesized live with OscillatorNode — zero audio files. The system log on the right reports real events as they happen, not simulated traffic.
